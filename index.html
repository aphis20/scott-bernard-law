About Scott V. Bernard, Esq.

Scott V. Bernard, Esq., has 23 years of experience protecting your rights across all Family Law Matters in Buffalo, NY.

He focuses his practice on all aspects of Family Law, including but not limited to Child Custody and Visitation, Child Support issues, Abuse and Neglect actions under Article 10, and Family Offenses.

He is a member of the New York State Bar Association and The Bar Association of Erie County.

Scott earned his law degree at The State University of New York Buffalo School of Law, finishing in the top 1/3 of his class. He earned his B.S. at State University of New York College at Buffalo in Sociology with a minor in Writing, summa cum laude.
